# Kerem Duymus

**Historian & Sociologist**

I work on the global history of the nineteenth century from the perspective of Africa and
West Asia: Ottoman–African relations, the Sahara, and the global history of Africa. My research is
archival — Turkey, Egypt, Libya, Niger, Chad, Nigeria, and Mali — and multilingual, drawing
on sources in Turkish, Arabic, Hausa, Tamasheq, and Fulfulde alongside European materials.

## Research interests

- Ottoman history and Ottoman–African relations
- The Sahara and trans-Saharan trade
- Sokoto Caliphate, Borno, and Ouaddaï
- Decolonial historiography and epistemologies
- Global history of Africa

 ## Open access

All my open-access publications — articles, theses, abstracts and bibliographic data —
are collected in the [**publications**](https://github.com/keremduymus/publications) repository.

My website is available in six languages: English, Türkçe, Français, Español, العربية, Hausa.
